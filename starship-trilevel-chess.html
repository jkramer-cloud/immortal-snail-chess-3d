Move an attack platform

Instead of moving a chess piece, you may shift an attack platform by one level if it holds exactly one of your pieces. The piece rides with it. A move cannot leave your king in check.

How to play

White starts on the lower level; Black starts on the upper level. Three fixed 4×4 platforms and four movable 2×2 attack platforms make 64 squares. Pieces follow normal chess directions as viewed from overhead; they may switch platforms along valid paths.

Classroom movement rule: A sliding piece may cross one platform seam or overlap per move. Knights leap between levels. Kings move one projected square. Pawn promotions work; castling and en passant are disabled in this variant. Standard check, checkmate and stalemate apply.

Not official tournament rules: The original TV series did not define a complete rulebook. This approachable variant is designed to teach the geometry before more complex fan rule sets.

Seven-board chess

Explore three levels of strategy

In regular chess, every piece lives on the same board. Here, pieces move across three fixed main boards and four small attack platforms. The same square can be vertically above or below another square.

Choose a guided demonstration to watch legal moves across the stacked boards, or jump straight into a two-player game.
