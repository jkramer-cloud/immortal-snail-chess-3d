How to survive

Move your chess pieces normally. Every three complete rounds, both immortal snails crawl one square toward the enemy king. They cannot be captured or blocked. Anything they reach is destroyed, including kings. Win by checkmate or catching the enemy king with a snail. Look out for the glowing warning squares before they move.
