How to survive

Chess rules include castling, en passant, promotion, and checks. Snails occupy squares like indestructible obstacles, so you cannot move onto or through them. Every three complete rounds, both immortal snails crawl one square toward the enemy king. They cannot be captured or blocked. Anything they reach is destroyed, including kings. Win by checkmate or catching the enemy king with a snail. Look out for the glowing warning squares before they move.
